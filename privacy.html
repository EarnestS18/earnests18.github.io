Privacy Policy

Buku Tamu is an internal tool used only by our own hotel outlets and administrators. It is not offered to the public.

The app connects to the owner's own Google Drive account solely to save daily and monthly report files and database backups. It uses the limited "drive.file" permission, so it can only access files it created itself. It does not read, share or sell any other Google data.

No data is shared with third parties. Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
